Key business dates

Tax and filing deadlines, the Union Budget and the festive season: dates to plan cash and stock around. They apply to most businesses, so they are not filtered by level.

  1. 20 Oct – 8 Nov 2026Next one. Usually October or November.

    Dussehra to Diwali festive buying season

    Government Gazetted holidays: Department of Personnel & Training (DoPT), Government of India

    Where: All India

    Peak sales weeks for FMCG, vehicles, durables and gifts: stock, credit and staffing plans for traders and dealers revolve around these dates.

    1 Trader2 Dealer Official site

    About this date

    DoPT list of Central Government holidays 2026: Dussehra Tue 20 Oct 2026, Diwali Sun 8 Nov 2026 (Delhi). Navratri and Dhanteras dates vary by region and are not on the gazetted list.

  2. 31 Oct 2026Next one. Usually April or October.

    MSME Form-1 half-yearly return (payments delayed beyond 45 days)

    Government Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)

    Where: All India

    If you supply bigger companies, this is when they have to report what they still owe you: a good time to follow up on overdue payments.

    3 Manufacturer Official site

    About this date

    Companies that owe micro/small suppliers for more than 45 days must report by 31 Oct (Apr-Sep half) and 30 Apr (Oct-Mar half). Next due 31 Oct 2026, then 30 Apr 2027.

  3. 15 Dec 2026Next one. Usually June, September, December or March.

    Advance tax instalments

    Government Income Tax Department (CBDT)

    Where: All India

    Cash-flow dates every owner should put on the calendar so tax payments don't squeeze working capital.

    1 Trader2 Dealer3 Manufacturer Official site

    About this date

    Income-tax Act 2025 s.408 / incometax.gov.in: cumulative 15% by 15 Jun, 45% by 15 Sep, 75% by 15 Dec, 100% by 15 Mar (presumptive-scheme taxpayers: full amount by 15 Mar). Next instalment 15 Dec 2026, then 15 Mar 2027.

  4. 31 Dec 2026

    GST annual return (GSTR-9 / GSTR-9C) due date

    Government CBIC / GST Council (GSTN portal)

    Where: All India

    Year-end check of all GST filings: mismatches with suppliers or dealers show up here, so traders and dealers should reconcile before the deadline.

    1 Trader2 Dealer Official site

    About this date

    CGST Rule 80 / GST portal FAQ: due by 31 December after the financial year (FY 2025-26 return due 31 Dec 2026) unless CBIC extends by notification. Mandatory above Rs 2 crore turnover; GSTR-9C above Rs 5 crore.

  5. Usually FebruaryDate not yet announced

    Union Budget

    Government Ministry of Finance, Government of India

    Where: New Delhi

    Changes to tax, customs duty, MSME credit and startup schemes take effect from 1 April: read it to reprice, re-plan and spot new schemes.

    1 Trader2 Dealer3 Manufacturer4 New products Official site

    About this date

    By convention presented on 1 Feb every year since 2017; the date for Budget 2027-28 has not been officially notified yet.
