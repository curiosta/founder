The business theory of levels

Everything here is organised around one idea: most businesses sit on one of four levels (trader, dealer, manufacturer, and new products and services), and each level makes money in a different way. Knowing your level helps you see where your profit really comes from and what it would take to move up. See the ladder.

How this site is written

  • Plain language. Business-school terms are avoided or explained the first time.
  • Indian examples, in rupees.
  • No invented facts. Examples with numbers are labelled Hypothetical.
  • Nothing here is legal, tax or financial advice for your specific situation. Check with a professional before signing agreements or making large commitments.
